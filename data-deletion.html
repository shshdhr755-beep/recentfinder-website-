User Data Deletion Instructions

RecentFinder — Last updated: 14 November 2026

The current version of RecentFinder does not provide user accounts or Instagram sign-in and does not intentionally store Instagram profile data, passwords, or access tokens.

Request deletion or ask a privacy question

If you believe RecentFinder has received or retained personal information about you, email Dilrajsain42@gmail.com with the subject “RecentFinder data deletion request”. Describe the information you want reviewed. Do not email your Instagram password or any access token.

We will review the request and respond. If we hold personal information that can be associated with your request, we will take appropriate steps to delete it unless we are required to retain it by law.

Instagram data

RecentFinder currently sends visitors to Instagram to view hashtag pages. Any information held by Instagram must be managed directly through Instagram and is subject to Instagram's own policies and controls.
