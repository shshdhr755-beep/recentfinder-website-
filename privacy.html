Privacy Policy

RecentFinder — Last updated: 14 November 2026

RecentFinder is a simple hashtag-discovery website. This policy describes the website as it currently operates.

Information we collect

The current version of RecentFinder does not ask visitors to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information. It does not intentionally collect or store Instagram passwords, access tokens, or profile information.

How the website works

When you enter a hashtag and choose to continue to Instagram, the website opens the corresponding Instagram hashtag page. Instagram is a separate service and its own privacy policy and terms apply to activity on Instagram.

Cookies and analytics

RecentFinder does not currently use its own advertising cookies or analytics tools. The hosting provider may process limited technical information as part of delivering and securing the website.

Third-party services

Links to Instagram take you to a third-party service. We do not control how Instagram collects or uses information. Please review Instagram's privacy policy.

Children

RecentFinder is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features change. Any new Instagram login, account connection, or data-processing feature will be described here before it is made available.
